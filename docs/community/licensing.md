DynaPlex is **free to use for everyone** — academic research, teaching,
and commercial applications alike, with no feature or usage limits.

## The engine

The DynaPlex engine ships as pre-built wheels on PyPI, licensed under the
DynaPlex License Agreement included in every wheel as `LICENSE.txt`. In
plain language:

- You may install and use DynaPlex **for any purpose**, royalty-free —
  there is no separate academic edition, no license server, and no limit
  on users, machines, cores, or problem sizes.
- The grant for each version you obtain is **perpetual and
  irrevocable**: once you have a version, your right to keep using it
  never expires. Results published with DynaPlex stay reproducible.
- You may redistribute unmodified wheels free of charge — for example on
  an institutional mirror, or archived alongside a paper for artifact
  evaluation.
- **Everything you build with DynaPlex is yours**: your models, your
  code, your trained policies, your simulation results, your
  publications. The license claims no rights over them.
- Like virtually all software licenses (open-source ones included),
  DynaPlex comes **as is, without warranty or liability**: you use it
  at your own risk, and validating results before you rely on them in
  decisions or publications is your responsibility.

The wheels bundle permissively licensed open-source components (LLVM,
pybind11, Boost.Math, and others); their licenses and copyright notices
ship in every wheel as `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`.

This plain-language summary is for orientation; the `LICENSE.txt` in the
wheel is the governing text.
